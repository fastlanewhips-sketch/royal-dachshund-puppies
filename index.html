Welcome To Our World

Small Paws, Royal Hearts

At Royal Dachshund Puppies, we appreciate everything that makes Dachshunds special: their distinctive appearance, spirited personalities, intelligence and devotion to their families.

Our goal is to help prospective owners make informed decisions about Dachshund companionship. We value honest communication, responsible ownership, appropriate veterinary care and the lifelong well-being of every dog.

Whether you are learning about the breed for the first time or preparing to welcome a Dachshund into your home, we invite you to explore our resources and contact us with your questions.

Meet The Breed

What Makes Dachshunds Special?

Learn about the different Dachshund coat varieties and the care each dog may need.

🐾

Smooth-Haired

Recognized for a short, sleek coat and a distinctive, expressive face. Routine grooming helps keep the coat healthy.

🐶

Long-Haired

Known for flowing hair and feathering around the ears, chest and tail. Regular brushing helps prevent tangles.

🐕

Wire-Haired

Recognized by a rougher coat, characteristic facial furnishings and an alert expression.

Health & Veterinary Care

Work with a veterinarian on vaccinations, preventive care, nutrition and age-appropriate health checks.

⌂

A Safe, Loving Home

Provide a comfortable environment, gentle handling, positive socialization and a consistent routine.

✦

Protect Their Backs

Dachshunds can be prone to spinal problems. Ask your veterinarian about safe handling, weight management and suitable exercise.

A Lifelong Commitment

Choose Your Companion Thoughtfully

Bringing a puppy home is an important decision. Ask questions, review available veterinary records, learn about the puppy's background and make sure you are prepared for the time, attention and expenses that responsible dog ownership requires.

We encourage prospective owners to verify breeder information and never rush a decision or payment.
